## 1. Who the game is for

iPlay Cornhole is for adults. **You must be 18 or older to play.** The game asks you to confirm your age when you first open it. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child is using the game, tell us at [contact email] and we will remove the account.

## 3. Voice chat

The game has live voice chat, provided by **Unity Vivox**. Your voice goes to the other players in your match through Vivox, not through our server. **Voice is not filtered or recorded by us.** Vivox has its own data practices (see [Unity's privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)). To let Vivox connect you, we give it a short-lived access token and your account id as your voice display name. You can turn voice off in Settings, mute your microphone, and block anyone: people you block are muted on your phone.

## 4. Ads

The game is free and earns money from **ads**, shown by **AppLovin MAX** and the ad networks connected to it [list the networks you enable]. Ads are never shown during a match. To show ads, these services collect **device and advertising identifiers, usage data, and an approximate location derived from your IP address**, and share it with advertisers. On iPhone we ask permission before tracking you across other apps; in the European Economic Area and the UK we ask for your consent first. You can change your choice in your phone's settings. See [AppLovin's privacy policy](https://www.applovin.com/privacy/).

## 5. Who can see what

- **Other players in your match** see your display name, your seat and character, and your account id (a random number the game uses for blocking and reporting).
- **Everyone signed in** can see the **public leaderboards**: your display name, rating, games, wins, losses and streak, **if** you have played at least 10 ranked games and did not turn off "Show me on the leaderboards" in your Profile.
- Anyone who has a match code can watch that match and see the same as its players.
- Our staff can look up accounts, reports and bans to keep players safe.
- We do not sell your information.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account:** Profile > Delete my account. It immediately erases your account, sign-in links, sessions, stats, ratings and blocks. No app? Ask at [your-domain]/delete-account and we will do it within 30 days.
- **Get a copy of your data:** Profile > Download my data.
- **Change your name** or **hide from the leaderboards** in your Profile.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have more rights, such as access, correction, deletion, objecting to processing, or complaining to a regulator. Contact us at [contact email] and we will help.
